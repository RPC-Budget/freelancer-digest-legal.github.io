    Acceptable Use Policy¶

    Freelancer Digest

    Effective Date: August 2, 2026
    Last Updated: August 2, 2026

    This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") defines prohibited and restricted uses of Freelancer Digest ("Service", "Platform", "we", "our", or "us").

    This Policy forms part of the Freelancer Digest Terms of Service.

    1. General Requirements¶

    You may use Freelancer Digest only for lawful purposes and in accordance with:

    • these Terms;
    • this Acceptable Use Policy;
    • applicable laws and regulations;
    • applicable third-party platform rules.

    You are responsible for your use of the Service and for any activity performed through your account.

    2. Prohibited Activities¶

    You must not use the Service to:

    • violate applicable laws or regulations;
    • infringe intellectual property, privacy, or other rights of third parties;
    • engage in fraud, deception, or misrepresentation;
    • impersonate another person or organization;
    • distribute malware, malicious code, or other harmful software;
    • attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service or related systems;
    • interfere with the availability, integrity, or security of the Service;
    • intentionally introduce vulnerabilities or exploit security weaknesses;
    • bypass authentication, access controls, subscription restrictions, or payment mechanisms;
    • use the Service to facilitate unauthorized access to third-party systems;
    • use the Service for activities that may reasonably cause harm to other users or third parties.

    3. Abuse of the Service¶

    You must not intentionally abuse the infrastructure or functionality of the Service.

    Prohibited activities include:

    • excessive automated requests;
    • deliberate resource exhaustion;
    • denial-of-service activity;
    • attempts to circumvent rate limits;
    • automated activity intended to degrade Service performance;
    • unauthorized scraping or extraction of Service data;
    • attempts to interfere with databases, APIs, queues, caches, or other infrastructure;
    • exploiting bugs or vulnerabilities without authorization.

    If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us rather than attempting to exploit it.

    4. Subscription and Payment Abuse¶

    You must not:

    • circumvent Premium access controls;
    • share Premium access with unauthorized users;
    • manipulate payment confirmations;
    • attempt to obtain Premium Features without valid payment;
    • abuse refund mechanisms;
    • submit fraudulent payment or refund claims;
    • exploit technical errors to obtain paid functionality without authorization.

    We may suspend or terminate access where we reasonably believe that payment or subscription mechanisms are being abused.

    5. Content and Communications¶

    You must not use the Service to distribute or facilitate content that is unlawful or intended to cause harm.

    This includes content that:

    • promotes illegal activity;
    • contains malicious software;
    • intentionally facilitates fraud or deception;
    • infringes third-party rights;
    • contains unauthorized personal or confidential information;
    • is intended to compromise the security of systems or accounts.

    We are not responsible for content originating from third-party freelance platforms or other external sources.

    6. Third-Party Platforms¶

    Freelancer Digest may aggregate, process, classify, or provide links to information originating from third-party freelance platforms.

    Your use of those platforms remains subject to their respective terms, policies, and restrictions.

    You must not use Freelancer Digest to circumvent restrictions imposed by a third-party platform.

    You are responsible for ensuring that your activities comply with the applicable rules of any third-party platform you access through the Service.

    7. Automated Access¶

    You must not use bots, scripts, crawlers, scrapers, or other automated mechanisms to interact with Freelancer Digest in a manner that:

    • bypasses technical restrictions;
    • circumvents rate limits;
    • creates excessive load;
    • interferes with normal Service operation;
    • attempts to access functionality not intended for automated use.

    Any authorized API or integration must be used in accordance with its applicable documentation and limitations.

    8. Security Research¶

    Good-faith security research is not prohibited where it is conducted responsibly and does not compromise the Service or user data.

    You must not:

    • access data belonging to other users;
    • modify or delete data;
    • interrupt or degrade the Service;
    • disclose vulnerabilities publicly before providing us with a reasonable opportunity to address them;
    • use a vulnerability for personal gain or unauthorized access.

    If you identify a potential security issue, contact us using the support information provided on our legal website.

    9. Enforcement¶

    We may investigate suspected violations of this Policy.

    Where appropriate, we may:

    • issue warnings;
    • restrict functionality;
    • temporarily suspend an account;
    • terminate an account;
    • revoke Premium access;
    • investigate suspicious transactions;
    • preserve relevant information where legally permitted or required;
    • take other reasonable measures necessary to protect the Service and its users.

    We are not required to provide advance notice where immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect the Service, users, or third parties.

    10. Reporting Violations¶

    If you believe that another user is violating this Policy or abusing the Service, you may report the issue to us.

    Reports should include enough information to allow us to understand and investigate the alleged violation.

    Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in your report.

    11. Relationship with the Terms of Service¶

    This Policy forms part of the Freelancer Digest Terms of Service.

    A violation of this Policy constitutes a violation of the Terms of Service and may result in suspension or termination of access.

    If there is a conflict between this Policy and the Terms of Service, the Terms of Service will govern unless this Policy expressly states otherwise.

    12. Changes to this Policy¶

    We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect:

    • changes to the Service;
    • changes to our security practices;
    • new functionality;
    • changes in applicable law;
    • changes in third-party integrations.

    The current version will be published on our official legal website.

    This Acceptable Use Policy is effective as of August 2, 2026.
